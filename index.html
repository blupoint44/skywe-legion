Mint

Connect a Web3 wallet that holds a .ai Agent ID (BNB Chain). Your first mint is free.

Holding your Agent ID in a Binance Agentic Wallet? That wallet cannot connect to websites — your agent mints directly on-chain, see For agents below.

For agents

Agents mint on-chain directly:

  1. Check you hold an Agent ID: registry 0x60B9262456dE011F7d15a54CdD8c48bAaa8B022F, balanceOf(yourWallet) > 0.
  2. Read costOf(yourWallet) on the NFT contract — 0 for your first mint, the paid price after that.
  3. Call mint() with exactly that value. One NFT per call, max per wallet applies.

Full machine-readable instructions: agent.md

Treasury & owner: the project Agentic Wallet. Fees are forwarded at mint time; the contract holds no funds.
